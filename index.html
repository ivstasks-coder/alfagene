About AlfaGene

A seed-stage company built on human epithelial/stem cell biology

AlfaGene Bioscience is a seed-stage biotechnology company focused on human epithelial/stem cell systems for disease modeling, target validation, and translational drug discovery. The company's foundational work centers on segment-resolved human gastrointestinal epithelial/stem cell biology and patient-matched tumor-normal systems.

AlfaGene's mission is to help oncology and translational research teams identify targets with stronger therapeutic windows by comparing tumor-derived epithelial cells directly against matched normal human epithelial counterparts.

Physician-scientist backgrounds · expertise in human GI epithelial/stem cell systems · scientific publications and patents.

Technology differentiators

What the platform does that tumor-only systems cannot

01
Patient-Matched Tumor-Normal Comparison
Direct comparison of tumor-derived and adjacent-normal epithelial/stem cells from the same patient and anatomical segment.
02
Segment-Resolved Human GI Biology
Human epithelial/stem cell resources representing defined gastrointestinal segments.
03
Cryopreserved, Expandable, Regrowable Cell Banks
Unlike one-time fresh tissue or short-lived explant systems, AlfaGene's resources are maintained as cryopreserved cell banks that can be thawed, expanded, and regrown for repeatable experiments.
04
Controlled Pure Epithelial/Stem Cell Populations
The platform provides a controlled epithelial/stem cell system for standardized target-screening workflows.

Oncology applications

Applications in Solid Tumor Target Validation

Solid tumor therapies often fail because targets identified in tumor-biased systems are also expressed in normal tissues, narrowing the therapeutic window and increasing the risk of on-target/off-tumor toxicity.

Colorectal Cancer

Current proof-of-concept using paired tumor and adjacent-normal epithelial/stem cell resources.

Gastrointestinal Cancers

Potential application across GI epithelial malignancies where tumor-normal comparison is important.

Broader Epithelial Solid Tumors

The workflow may be extendable to other epithelial solid tumors, including lung cancer, through appropriate tumor/adjacent-normal sample partnerships.

Therapeutic modalities supported
ADC target validation/Bispecific antibody target selection/Immune engager target de-risking/CAR-T/TCR-like target safety assessment/Companion diagnostic biomarker prioritization/Normal epithelial liability screening
